HOW THE CITY WORKS

Move one block, or wait. Every action triggers the displayed sweep. Orange blocks will be scanned; covered blocks are safe. Pick up the parcel, then reach the antenna before your turns run out. Buildings block movement.

Use the arrow keys or WASD after starting, tap an adjacent block, or use the direction buttons. Space waits while the map has focus. Invalid moves cost no turns. The next sweep is always visible.

AUTHOR’S NOTE / 001

A city is more than its official record. This is a game about carrying the small things that keep people in it. Each contract asks you to save something too ordinary to count as a revolution.
